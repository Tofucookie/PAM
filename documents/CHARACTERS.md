# Minor Characters

## Blue Venus Fly Trap

A refence to Dullpepper. Used to be involved with the Fish Gang. Not even involved with the Pumpkins vs Melon conflict.


## Fish Gang

A reference to Pumpki's Fish Gang, the late night VC group. They are five fishes. They are an emodiment of what people imagine what a 90s high school gang looks like, however they are on your side and you encounter them from time to time, similar to the Toad Brigade encounters from Super Mario Galaxy. 

### Sun Fish

Reference to Baconator / Sunfish. The main fish of the crew. He is a tall big fish who wears sunglasses and loves bacon!

### Forge Fish

Reference to Drakenforge. A Blacksmith-like character; the nerdiest & weirdest one of them all! Walks head facing down and flops around a lot, unlike other fish walking formally. He wears eye glasses and is good at forging items together. Forge Fish goes rogue - his medication goes to his head. Also likes to spout random nonsense that might connect to other things outside The World

### Slime Fish

Reference to Anglerfish. Slime fish will be a thing. 

### Sheep Fish

Reference to FlubbedSheep. A fish known with wool with a mash!

## The Rogue Messenger

A reference to RogueWatcher03. He was behind the pipebomb found in your mailbox.
