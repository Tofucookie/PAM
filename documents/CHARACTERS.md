# Characters
The Pumpki game features many characters from the Pumpkiverse.

# Major Charactors


## That Guy

The silent protagonist of the game who you can name, but most people just call him “That Guy”. In this world of interesting, fun characters, each unique from the last, That Guy is always silent, keeps to himself, and is considered the most boring person in Pumpville or whatever the town is called. But ever since the town got destroyed by the Watermelons, everyone is too scared to do anything except for That Guy who decides to fight them off not from the goodness of his heart, not for any awards, but because he has nothing better to do.

# Minor Characters

## Fish Gang

A reference to Pumpki's Fish Gang, the late night VC group. They are five fishes. They are an emodiment of what people imagine what a 90s high school gang looks like, however they are on your side and you encounter them from time to time, similar to the Toad Brigade encounters from Super Mario Galaxy.

# Creatures

## Woodlens

Creatures in the Hollow Woods that live in the Timber Village led by Master Wood. They’re completely disconnected from the outside world, so much so that they don’t even know about any other species other than the ones in the forest. They may seem dumb on the outside, but they are wise beings who are blessed by a magical force that has protected them for centuries. Near the end of the game, they could bless you with their power to defeat Lord Pumpki
