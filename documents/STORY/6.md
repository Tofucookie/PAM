# Chapter 6 - Surfing the Interwebs

 You are introduced to Jack Bocks in this chapter. You must be the first to defeat Jack Bocks as he tries to kill you in the internet. You'll have to fight for your sanity as you suffer through shitty jokes, stupid people, and all of the things you'll find on the internet. At the end of the chapter, you'll fight Jack Bocks and it's revealed that he is the spider that connects the whole Internet together and is the reason why the Internet is such a shitty place. When you defeat Jack, you realize that the internet cannot be changed, and the damage is and forever will be done, and there's nothing you can do to make the internet a better place.
