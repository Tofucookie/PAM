# Chapter 2 - A City Mystery

A chapter that takes place in a city and involves a bunch of mystery. You travel across the city and do battles on board buses and trains to solve a mystery.

After doing so, the player exits via a toilet in a random house. Upon leaving, an NPC standing outside the bathroom says:

> "Thank you for using my bathroom!"

This is a reference to Canadians thanking bathroom users.
