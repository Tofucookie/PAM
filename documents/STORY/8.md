# Chapter 8 - The End of the World
 The final chapter of the game. at the end of the game, all of the people you meet give you your power, including the fish and the Woodlens, and Master Wood tells you that you will fail, but since Wood always tells the opposite of the future, you think you’ll succeed. But when you fight Lord Pumpki, it turns out he was right. But all of sudden, all of the powers of the people all power you up and save you.

## Mr. Tips & The Final Boss ##
 Right before the final boss, Mr. Tips from chapter one makes a re-appearance and gives the player a tutorial on mechanics they already know:

 *Mr Tips, in chapter 8:*
 > "Ah! I finally got this phone to work. These whipper-snapper devices are so convoluted. To interact with items, simply press <KEY>!

 *Party Member:*
 > "Why is Mr. Tips calling you NOW? Did he really take that long?"
 
Fortunately, he gives crucial information to defeat the bosses:
 
 *Mr Tips, in the middle of the final boss:*
 > "One more thing! Attack <boss> by doing <sequence>!"
 
 *Party Member:*
 > "Wait! Mr. Tips is right! Try it!!!"

## Final Boss: 2nd Phase

 After defeating him, Lord Pumpki unleashes a final magical spell which summons Green Ninja to a much more stronger and evil form. He leaves your party for the rest of the battles and now has the ability to transform to become past foes you fought.
 
## Final Boss: Final Phase
 
 The rogue messenger was behind this all along and is the final boss. However, mid-way into the fight, your friends form a power circle and give you a gun. Suddenly the rogue messager immediately dies.
 
## Epilogue
 
 The boss is defeated.
