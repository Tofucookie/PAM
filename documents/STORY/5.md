# Chapter 5 - The Hollow Night

 It is rumored that in the heart of the mysterious Hollow Woods, lies an ancient village inhabited by wood creatures known as Woodlens. You head out on a journey to the Hollow Woods. It is dark and scary, monsters lurking at every corner, and you’re completely lost. After what seems like hours, you meet a little Woodlen named Treep. He also seems to be lost, but at least knows the area, definitely more than you do. So you follow Treep protecting him from monsters, when you finally find the Timber Village. You look around the village going to shops and rest for a bit. Then, you meet the leader, Master Wood. He tells you that the village needs your help to defeat a tree monster known as “The Log”. But when he looks into the future, he predicts that you will fail, but you go on ahead anyways. After a long battle, you head back to the village and meet Master Wood. You question if he actually saw the future, and he responds “I do not tell lies, nor do I tell the truth. Only he who is wise enough can understand my foolishness.” And this concludes chapter 5 “The Hollow Night” but you can always fast travel to the Timber Village to buy gear, rest up, ect. And you can talk to Master Wood to tell you about your fortune. He’ll tell you if you’re prepared for the next area and boss based on the gear you have, kinda like the old man who summons skeletron. If you are prepared, he will say you will fail, if you aren’t, he will say you will fail
