# Chapter 3 - The Promise


## Learning About Lord Pumpki

The king, Lord Pumpki, vows to one day kill all of the Melons to bring back the glory of the Pumpkins. So he sets off on a journey to become more powerful, and finds the Sacred Vine, a whip so powerful it can kill even the most powerful of beings in one swing. But this power comes at a cost, whoever takes the Vine will be under its control, and when Lord Pumpki took the whip, only evil thoughts ran through his head, and instead of rebuilding his kingdom, he wanted to enslave the world.


## SIDE EVENTS

### Pipebomb in the Mailbox - Pipe Bomb Ending
 A mailbox is found in the new areas. There is a 1/200 chance interacting with it will reveal there is a pipe bomb in the mailbox. The player is given an option to open it, and selecting yes kills them and brings them to a game over scene, and credits roll while mocking the player. It turns out the Rogue Messenger is behind this. Suggested by @Doodle#9567 and @Born of Pixels#6462.
