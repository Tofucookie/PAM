# Major & Main Locations
## Slickstone Island - Ancient Mushroom Society
Not much is known about the Ancient Mushroom Society, since according to the Slickstone Chronicles, they turned on each other and drove themselves into extinction. All that is known about the mysterious society is that the land they reside in is where all life on (name of world) originated. We later learn that the people of this civilisation had been experimenting with gene manipulation because they were a highly advanced people who were capable of creating lands that defied gravity itself. 

The protagonist goes to this island at somepoint and a lot of revelations happen. They learn of the atrocities of the Inner Stem (which was essentially a secret team that were purposed with finding more creative and morally questionable methods of creating a perfect race of mushrooms). This in turn lead to an outbreak of a virus that caused a mutation within the mushroom people that altered their appearances into Pumpkins and Melons and this drastically changed the brain chemistry of the mushrooms with Pumkins being far different in personality and world view from the other  despite them remaining peaceful. Some leaders of mushroom tribes and people in general went insane and they waged war on each other and it is revealed that the war of the ancient ages is the same war as the great pumpkin-melon war. 

On this Island in the depths of the caves and mines, we find the last surviving pure Mushroom, this mushroom is an Elder Mushroom, who were usually the ones that would carry and preserve the race's history and they generally live for centuries. This Mushroom becomes the key to reversing the virus that corrupted the once great civilization...

## Hollow Woods - The Timber Village
A dark forest that can feel like it goes on forever with dangerous monsters like wolfs, goblins, tree monsters, ect. And at the heart of the forest lies the Timber Village, with Woodlens, ancient wood creatures, who live in treehouses with clan leader, Master Wood. Unfortunately, one of Lord Pumpki’s plan is to cut down the entire forest and kill the Woodlens.
