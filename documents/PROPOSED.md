## Quests & Events
  * Lottery Ending: 1 in 90,000 chance to win the lottery; you beat the game & get Steam achievement if you get this
  * Character is approached by a citizen
  * Citizen talks about a recent attack by the melons and talks how he was once a brave warrior but now is reduced to nothing more than a sickly old man 
  * Citizen then makes a realization “You would make an excellent warrior for this land. Would you listen to an old man’s ramble?” 
  * Player gets a choice of yes or no if player picks no citizen will say “Wow…. Seriously? Well……… This is awkward”    
  * If a player says yes citizen will say “Magnificent come with me and to the old training grounds there we shall begin” 
  * Tutorial begins
